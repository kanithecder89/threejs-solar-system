1. Who We Are

Ozone Space is a student-focused space education platform built to make learning about space science and STEM more practical, accessible, and engaging. We connect students with real knowledge, tools, and guidance bridging the gap between curiosity and career in space exploration.

We are proud to share that Ozone Space EdTech & Application Pvt. Ltd. is now officially approved as a Registered Space Tutor by ISRO, further strengthening our commitment to delivering credible and high-quality space education across India.

×

2. Our Mission

To provide quality, hands-on space education and STEM programs for school and college students, helping them explore science beyond textbooks and build future-ready skills.

×

3. Our Vision

To inspire the next generation of space leaders and innovators by making advanced learning in astronomy, robotics, and space science available to every learner.

×

4. Our Journey

Ozone Space started as a small initiative to bring space science closer to classrooms. What began with local workshops and stargazing sessions in Lucknow has now expanded into a national effort with a mission to make space and STEM learning more interactive, affordable, and inspiring. We've worked closely with educators, engineers, and ISRO experts to shape programs that students genuinely connect with.

×

5. What We Offer

We offer a variety of programs designed to make space education hands-on, engaging, and relevant for today's learners. From interactive online courses to school workshops and stargazing events, our sessions blend theory with real-world application. Our content focuses on nurturing scientific thinking, creativity, and problem-solving ensuring that students not only learn about space but also how to explore it like real scientists and engineers.

Whether you're interested in space science, robotics, astronomy, or astrobiology, Ozone Space provides a platform to explore, experiment, and grow.

7. Our Impact & Vision Forward

Our vision extends far beyond education. Ozone Space is committed to becoming Rajasthan's first space technology company, actively contributing to the space applications sector in India. From igniting curiosity in young minds through space education to developing real-world solutions in satellite-based services, remote sensing, and aerospace communication technologies, we are laying the groundwork to shape the future of space tech in India.

We believe that nurturing students, empowering STEM educators, and collaborating with forward-thinking schools, institutions, and government programs will help us build a stronger, more inclusive and innovative Indian space ecosystem — right from the grassroots.
